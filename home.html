HOW IT WORKS

Give TermRunway a financial picture. It turns that picture into a runway.

Start with the numbers you already know. The current application then uses them to calculate your forward financial position.

01

Choose a planning method

Use Semester for whole-period figures or Monthly for recurring monthly amounts.

02

Enter money coming in

Add the money already available now and the income you expect during the planning period.

03

Plan your expenses

Estimate costs such as rent, food, transport, utilities, entertainment, books, and other spending.

04

Set the timeline

Choose your start and end dates so the forecast is tied to an actual period instead of just a total.

YOUR DASHBOARD

The numbers answer the questions that matter.

₹

Projected balance

Shows the money TermRunway projects to remain at the end of your planning period after expected future income and remaining planned costs.

↗

Daily safe spending

Converts a positive projected balance into a daily figure for the remaining days.

%

Plan health

Gives you a quick view of the buffer in the current financial plan.

◌

Charts & analysis

See forecast movement, cash flow, expense distribution, and planned-versus-actual spending.
