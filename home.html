HOW IT WORKS

Start with today. Think through the whole term.

TermRunway is built around a simple idea: a budget is more useful when it shows what today's choices mean for tomorrow.

01

Set the timeline

Choose the planning period with start and end dates, using a semester or monthly approach.

02

Add money coming in

Enter what is already available and the income you expect during the period.

03

Plan the costs

Account for recurring and planned expenses such as food, rent, transport, utilities, books, and more.

04

Read the runway

See projected balance, remaining days, spending patterns, and the room your plan leaves for decisions.
